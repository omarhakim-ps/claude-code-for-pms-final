What to measure

Why this order

  • Alert first: it doesn't touch ranking, so it fixes the silent failure without muddying later measurements.
  • Wait before penalty: fixing the wait should remove most misses; the penalty change then shows what it adds on its own.
  • Penalty before recovery: otherwise misses keep pushing down the scores being restored.
  • Recovery before local routing: this shows how much of the local-calls problem recovery solves, so step 7 only fixes what's left.
  • Retry and widening last: both add pings, which would blur earlier results.

Where to look in step 5

  • The real 4.2 release diff and production settings; this repo has one commit.
  • available_for and callout.region: who is on the list.
  • travel_time_minutes: did estimates change around 12 Aug?
  • score(), replayed with old and new weights, and the 45-minute cutoff.
  • _scores: how production stores it, and whether it reset at release.
  • push_to_device, then the phone app: why out-of-area pings started being taken.

Before starting

  • Agree the miss penalty and recovery speed with Wen.

  • Tell Supply before any routing change.
  • Settings ship globally each month, so these are before/after comparisons unless a per-area switch is built.

Watch throughout

  • Time to fill a callout: a longer wait delays the next ping.
  • Acceptance: 66–73% back to 75–78%.
  • Tickets: 20–32 a week back to about 6–7.

What the model assumes

  • Misses: two observed points per group: 90s and 60s. Quiet four 2.9% and 64% (only 25 pings); everyone else 2.1% and 12.4%. Between and beyond them the curve is assumed; dashed lines mark extrapolation outside 60–90s.
  • "Misses caused by the wait": sets how much of the rise goes away with a longer wait. It's unproven, which is why step 1 logs answer times.
  • Who gets pinged: the quiet four's share of pings runs from 5% at score 0 to 28% when level with a rival at 0.9, based on the ranking formula's travel-time trade-off. That straight line is assumed.
  • Starting point: every preset starts from today, with the quiet four at score 0 and 5% of pings. "Before 4.2 settings" therefore shows the old settings applied now, not the period before 4.2.
  • Answers: 79% of answered pings are taken, as in both periods. Taken pings have no recorded answer time, so "seconds lost" counts only failed pings.
  • Unfilled callouts: 5.3% end on a turn-down whatever the settings; those ending on a miss scale with the miss rate. A retry succeeds at the same rate as any other ping.
  • Not modelled: the out-of-area jump, because its cause is unknown, so local-call rates aren't projected. Also holiday or callout volume changes.
