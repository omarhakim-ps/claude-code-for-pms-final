Two-minute walkthrough
  1. Send a local ping. It shows on all three screens with a 60s countdown.
  2. Nudge from Kip's console or phone. Meteor Mite's phone shakes.
  3. Let it run out. See the missed-ping notice, then the second chance.
  4. Click Meteor Mite on the console to see their pings and ranking over time.
  5. Apply one-time restore. Their ranking goes back to normal on every screen.
  6. Tap any ping to raise a support ticket about it.
What stays the same: the 60-second ping wait. Callouts filled and the quiet four's share come back in every scenario. Pings answered and taken depend on the new alerts working, which is why they ship first and get measured.
